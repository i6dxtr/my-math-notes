#### 2.2
Goal 1: Locate a basis for $\text{null}(A)$ 
- $\text{dim}(A)\rightarrow$ number of elements
$$
\begin{align}
A&& B=\text{ref}(A)  \\
\overline{x}\in \text{null}(A) && .. \\
A\overline{x}=\overline{0} \leftarrow && \rightarrow B\overline{x}=\overline{0} \\
\text{Conclude:} &&  \\
\text{null}(A)=\text{null}(B)
\end{align}
$$
