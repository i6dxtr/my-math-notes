### Scalar multiplication, Vector addition
- $\mathbb{R}^{n}:{ \langle x_{1}, i, x_{a} \rangle, x_{1},...,x_{n} \in \mathbb{R}}$
- $M_{m},n$=$m\cdot n$ matrices with real entries
- Addition is component wise:
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
x_{1} \\
...  \\
x_{n}
\end{pmatrix}+
\begin{pmatrix}
y_{1} \\
... \\
y_{u}
\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}
x_{1}+y_{1} \\
... \\
x_{n}+y_{u}
\end{pmatrix}
$$
- Images composed of 3 matrices, respective to R, G, B in color spectrum
	- Each entry in matrix is an integer value $[0, 255]$
	- Size of each matrix the same as image dimensions respective to pixel #
- Vector addition $\longrightarrow$ controls contrast
	- Pixel value above mean = add to brightness
	- Pixel value below mean = opposite

### Matrices
- $A$ is an $m \times n$ matrix, means $A$ has $m$ rows and $n$ columns

> [!example]
> $$
> A=\begin{pmatrix}
> 3 & 7 \\
> 8 & 4  \\
> 1 & 2
> \end{pmatrix}\text{ is a }3 \times 2\text{ matrix}
> $$

- use NumPy notation:
	- $A[i,j]=$ the $ij$ entry of $A$
	- $A[0,1]=7$
	- $A[:,0]=$ "every *x*". *here, means every ==row==*
	-           = $\langle 3,8,1 \rangle$
	- $A[2, :]=$ $[1, 2]$

> [!example]
> $$
> \begin{align} B&=\begin{pmatrix}
> 1 & 2 & 3 \\
> 4 & 5 & 6 \\
> 7 & 8 & 9
> \end{pmatrix}  \\
> B[1:,0:2]&=\begin{pmatrix}
> 4 & 5 \\
> 7 & 8
> \end{pmatrix}\end{align}
> $$

### Matrix Multiplication
- $A$ is $7 \times6$
- $B$ is $6 \times101$
-  $AB=A@B$ is $7 \times101$
- $A$ is $m \times n$, $b$ is $n \times1$ matrix
- Then $Ab$ is a $m \times1$ matrix
#### General Formula
- $Ab=b[0]A[:,0]+b[1]A[:,1]+...+b[n-1]A[:,n-1]$

> [!example]
> $$
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 5 & 7 \\
> 4 & 1  \\
> 0 & -1
> \end{pmatrix} \times
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 2 \\
> 1
> \end{pmatrix} =
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 5\cdot2+7\cdot1 \\
> 4\cdot2+1\cdot1 \\
> 0\cdot2+-1\cdot1
> \end{pmatrix}
> $$
> - $A$ is $m \times n$, $B$ is $n \times K$
> - $AB[:, j]=\sum B[i,j]A[:,j]$
> $$
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 5 & 6 \\
> 2 & 1
> \end{pmatrix} \times
> \begin{pmatrix}
> -1 & 1 \\
> 0 & 1
> \end{pmatrix}=
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 5\cdot(-1)+6\cdot0 & 5\cdot1+6\cdot1 \\
> 2\cdot(-1)+1\cdot0 & 2\cdot1+1\cdot1
> \end{pmatrix}
> $$
