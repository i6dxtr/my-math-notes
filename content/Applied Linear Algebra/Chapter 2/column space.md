- the [[Subspaces|subspace]] of a [[Matrix|matrix]] spanned by its columns
	- take $\mathbf{v}_{1}, \mathbf{v}_{2}, ..., \mathbf{v}_{n}\in\mathbb{R}^{K}$
	- then $\text{span}(\mathbf{v}_{1}, \mathbf{v}_{2}, ..., \mathbf{v}_{n}\in\mathbb{R}^{K})=\left( a_{1}\mathbf{v}_{1}+\cdots+a\mathbf{v}_{n}:a_{1}, ..., a_{n}\in \mathbb{R} \right)$
		- def. of subspace of $\mathbb{R}^{n}$
- the other subspace typically observed is the [[row space]]

> [!definition]
> $$
> \begin{align}
> A&=\begin{pmatrix} \mathbf{v}_{1}&\mathbf{v}_{2}&\cdots&\mathbf{v}_{n} \end{pmatrix}\\ &\Longrightarrow \text{col}(A)=\text{span}\left\{ \mathbf{v}_{1}\ \mathbf{v}_{2}\ \cdots\ \mathbf{v}_{n} \right\}\subset \mathbb{R}^{n}
> \end{align}
> $$
