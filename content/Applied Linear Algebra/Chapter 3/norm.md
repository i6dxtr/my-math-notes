- also called the *Euclidean norm* or *length* of a vector, represented by the following notation:

> [!definition]
> $$\lvert \lvert \mathbf{v} \rvert  \rvert =\sqrt{\mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{v}}=\sqrt{v_{1}^{2}+\mathbf{v}_{2}^{2}+...+v_{n}^{2}}$$

![[Pasted image 20240922203748.png]]
- expressed as the [[Dot Product|dot product]] of a vector with itself
- every nonzero vector has a positive norm
- zero vector is the only vector with a 0 norm
