> [!theorem]
> The [[norm]] associated with an [[Dot Product|inner product]] satisfies the **triangle inequality**:
> $$
> > \begin{align}
> \lvert \lvert \mathbf{v}+\mathbf{w} \rvert  \rvert \leq \lvert \lvert \mathbf{v} \rvert  \rvert + \lvert \lvert \mathbf{w} \rvert  \rvert &&\text{for all }\mathbf{v}, \mathbf{w}\in V.
> \end{align}
>
> $$
> ... which holds *if and only if* $\mathbf{v}$ and $\mathbf{w}$ are parallel vectors.
