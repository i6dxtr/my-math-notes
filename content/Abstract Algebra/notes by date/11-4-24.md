### Chapter 16: Fundamental Homomorphism Theorem
==Recall==. for a homomorphism $f:G\rightarrow H$:
1. $\text{ker}( f )=\left\{ x\in G\ |\ f( x )=e_{H} \right\}$
2. $\text{ker}( f )$ is a normal subgroup of $G$
3. if $f$ is surjective, $H$ the *homomorphic image* of $G$
==Recall==. Every normal subgroup is a kernel

#### FHT

> [!theorem]
> Every homomorphic image of $G$ is isomorphic to a quotient of $G$. In particular, if $f:G\rightarrow H$ is a surjective homomorphism, then $H\cong G / K$ where $K=\text{ker}( f )$.

> [!corollary] Lemma.
> Let $F:G\rightarrow H$ be a homomorphsim. Let $K=\text{ker}( f )$. Let $a,b\in G$. Then $Ka=Kb$ *if and only if* $f( a )=f( b )$.

> [!example]
> ##### $f:GL_{n}( \mathbb{R} )\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{*}$
> 2. $f( A )=\text{det}( A )$
> 3. $K=\text{ker}( f )=SL_{n}( \mathbb{R} )$
> 4. by lemma, $SL_{n}( \mathbb{R} )A=SL_{n}( \mathbb{R} )B$ if and only if $\text{det}( A )=\text{det}( B )$
> 	1. cosets of $SL_{n}( \mathbb{R} )$ in $GL_{n}( \mathbb{R} )$ correspond to nonzero reals
> ###### $f:\mathbb{Z}\rightarrow Z_{n}$, $f( k )=k\text{ mod }n$
> 1. $K=\text{ker}( f )=\left\{ ...,-2n,-n,0,n,2n,... \right\}=\left\langle n \right\rangle$
> 2. thus, by lemma, $\left\langle n \right\rangle+a=\left\langle n \right\rangle+b$ if and only if $a\text{ mod }n=b\text{ mod }n$.
>
> #### Proof of FHT
> 1. Define $\Phi:G / K\rightarrow H$
> 	1. by $\Phi( Kx )=f( x )$
> 	2. note: $\Phi( Ka )=\Phi( Kb )$
> 	3. note: $\Phi$ an isomorphism from $G / K$ to $H$.
> 2. also, $\gamma: G\rightarrow G / K$
> 	1. by $\gamma( x )=Kx$
> 	2. see [[Drawing 2024-11-04 15.30.27.excalidraw|figure]]
> 3. We show:
> 	1. $\Phi$ is well-defined
> 		1. if $Ka=Kb$ then $f( a )=f( b )$
> 		2. so $\Phi( Ka )=\Phi( Kb )$, true by lemma
> 	2. $\Phi$ is injective
> 		1. if $\Phi ( Kb )$ then $f( a )=f( b )$
> 		2. so $Ka=Kb$, true by lemma
> 	3. $\Phi$ is surjective
> 		1. Let $h\in H$
> 		2. $f$ is surjective
> 			1. $\exists a\in G$ s.t. $f( a )=h$
> 		3. So $Ka\in G / H$ and $\Phi( Ka )=f( a )=h$
> 	4. $\Phi$ is a homomorphsim
> 		1. Let $Ka,Kb\in G / H$
> 		2. $\Phi( KaKb )=\Phi( Kab )$
> 			1. $=f( ab )$
> 			2. $=f( a )f( b )$, since $f$ is homomorphic
> 			3. $=\Phi ( Ka ) \Phi( Kb )$
> 	5. *Q.E.D*

> [!example]
> ##### Use FHT to compute $GL_n(\mathbb{R}) / SL_n(\mathbb{R})$
>    ... i.e. find a more familiar group it's isomorphic to
> 1. We find a homomorphism $f:GL_n(\mathbb{R})$ with $\text{ker}( f )=SL_n(\mathbb{R})$
> 	1. Define $f( A )=\text{det}( A )$
> 		1. $f:GL_n(\mathbb{R}) \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{*}$ is a surjective homomorphsim
> 		2. $\text{ker}( f )=SL_n(\mathbb{R})$
> 	2. thus, by FHT, $GL_n(\mathbb{R}) / SL_n(\mathbb{R}) \cong \mathbb{R}^{*}$
> ##### Use FHT to compute $\mathbb{Z} / \left\langle n \right\rangle$ where $n\in \mathbb{Z}^{+}$
> 1. Define $f:\mathbb{Z}\rightarrow\mathbb{Z}_{n}$ by $f( k )=k\text{ mod }n$
> 	1. $f$ is a surjective homomorphism
> 	2. $\text{ker}( f )=\left\langle n \right\rangle$
> 2. thus by FHT $\mathbb{Z} / \left\langle n \right\rangle\cong \mathbb{ Z}_{n}$
