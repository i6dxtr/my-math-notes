## Chapter 13
==recall.== if $H\subset G$, the *index* of $H$ in $G$ is $(G:H)$, defined as the number of distinct right cosets of $H\in G$.
- if $G (\rightarrow H)$ is finite, then $\left( G:H \right)=\lvert G \rvert /\lvert H \rvert$
- in $\infty$ case, count the cosets
*e.g.* $G=\mathbb{Z}, H=\left\langle 3 \right\rangle$
- $\left( \mathbb{Z}:\left\langle 3 \right\rangle \right)=3$, since can be rewritten $\mathbb{Z}+0, \mathbb{Z}+1, \mathbb{Z}+2$
*e.g.* $G=\mathbb{Q}, H\in \mathbb{Z}$
- $\left( \mathbb{Q}:\mathbb{Z} \right)\rightarrow \infty$
	- $\mathbb{Z}+0=\left\{ ....-2,-1,0,1,2,... \right\}$
	- $\mathbb{Z}+\frac{1}{2}=\left\{ ...,-\frac{3}{2}, -\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2}, \frac{3}{2}, \frac{5}{2} \right\}$
	- $\mathbb{Z}+\frac{1}{3}=\left\{ ...,-\frac{5}{3}, -\frac{2}{3}, \frac{1}{3}, \frac{4}{3}, \frac{7}{3}, ... \right\}$
	- $\mathbb{Z}+\frac{1}{4}$, etc...
	- $\mathbb{Z}+\frac{1}{n}, n\in\mathbb{Z}^{+}$ all different cosets
==note.== $\left( G:H \right)=$ the number of distance left cosets of $H$ in $G$
- b/c $f(Ha)=a^{-1}H$ is a well-defined bijection from $\left\{ Hx\ |\ x\in G \right\}$ to $\left\{ xH\ |\ x\in G \right\}$

## Chapter 15
==recall.== if $H$ is a *normal* subgroup of $G$, $G / H=\left\{ Ha\ |\ a\in G \right\}$ is a group.
- $(Ha)(Hb)=Hab$
==recall.== if $f:G\rightarrow G'$ a homomorphism, $\text{ker}(f)$ is a normal subgroup of $G$.
- conversely, every normal subgroup is a kernel of some homomorphsim
Let $H$ be a normal subgroup of $G$. Define $f:G\rightarrow G / H$ by $f(a)=Ha$
- *Claim.* $\text{ker}(f)=H$
- *Proof.* 
	- Let $a\in \text{ker}(f)$. WTS $a\in H$
		- So $f(a)=He$
		- So $Ha=He$
		- Then $a=ea\in Ha=He=H$
		- Therefore $a\in H$
	- Now let $a\in H$. WTS $a\in \text{ker}(f)$
		- $f(a)=Ha=He$
		- Since $a\in H=He$, $Ha=He$
