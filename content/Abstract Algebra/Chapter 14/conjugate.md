> [!definition]
> The expression $xax^{-1}$ is called a **conjugate** of $a$. It can be written as $x^{-1}ax=\left( x^{-1} \right)a\left( x^{-1} \right)^{-1}=yay^{-1}$, where $y=x^{-1}$, so $x^{-1}ax$ is also a *conjugate* of $a$. 

- a subgroup $H$ of $G$ is called [[normal subgroup|normal]] if it is closed under conjugates
