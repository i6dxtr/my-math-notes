> [!definition]
> Let $f:G\rightarrow H$ be a [[Homomorphism|homomorphism]]. The **kernel** of $f$ is the set $K$ of all the elements of $G$ which are carried by $f$ onto the neutral element of $H$. That is, $$K=\left\{ x\in  G\ |\ f(x)=e \right\}.$$

- is a [[normal subgroup]] of its superset

> [!remark]
> Let $f: G_{1}\rightarrow G_{2}$ be a homomorphism.
> Let $a\in \text{ker}(f)$ and $x\in G_{1}$.
> Then $xax^{-1}$ and $x^{-1}ax$ are in $\text{ker}(f)$ $\longrightarrow$
> 1. $f(xax^{-1})$
> 	1. $=f(x)f(a)f(x^{-1})$
> 	2. $=f(x)ef(x^{-1})$
> 	3. $=f(x)f(x^{-1})$
> 	4. $=f(x)f(x)^{-1}$
> 	5. $=e.$

- aka, $\text{ker}( f )$ is closed under [[conjugate|conjugates]].
