#ch2
properties of $GL_{n}(\mathbb{R})$:
- invertibility
	- $\forall A\in GL_{n}(\mathbb{R})\ \text{det}(A)\neq 0$
- [[operation associativity|associativity]]
- existence of an identity element
	- the identity matrix $I_{n}$
- existence of inverses
	- $\forall A\in GL_{n}(\mathbb{R})\ \exists A^{-1}$ s.t. $A\cdot A^{-1}=A^{-1}\cdot A=I$
- closure under multiplication
	- product of two invertible matrices is also invertible
- closure under scalar multiplication
