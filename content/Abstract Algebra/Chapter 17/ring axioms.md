- All [[Rings|rings]] have a set of facts about them that always hold true:

> [!corollary] Ring Axioms
> 1. $a\cdot 0=0$
> 2. $a\cdot( -b )=-( a\cdot b )$
> 3. $( -a )\cdot( -b )=a\cdot b$

> [!proof]
> ##### Proving 1.)
> 1. WTS $a\cdot0$ satisfies the definition of an identity element
> 	1. Cancellation law: $a+b=a+c\longrightarrow b=c$
> 	2. Take $a\cdot a+0=a\cdot a$ ($0$ is the identity for $+$)
> 		1. $=a\cdot ( a+0 )$
> 		2. $=a\cdot a+ a\cdot 0$ (distribution)
> 		3. $=a\cdot 0$ (cancellation)
> ##### Proving 2.)
> 1. WTS $a\cdot( -b )$ and $a\cdot b$ are additive inverses
> 	1. $a\cdot (  -b )+ a \cdot b$
> 		1. $=a \cdot (  ( -b ) + b )$ (distribution)
> 		2. $= a \cdot 0$ (definition of subtraction)
> 		3. $= 0.$ (by $1.$)
