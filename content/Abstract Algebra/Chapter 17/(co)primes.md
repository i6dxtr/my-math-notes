> [!theorem]
> If $m$ and $n$ are coprime, then the ring $\mathbb{Z}_{m} \times \mathbb{Z}_{n}$ is *isomorphic* to the ring $\mathbb{Z}_{mn}$

> [!proof]
> 1. Define $f:\mathbb{Z}_{mn}\rightarrow\mathbb{Z}_{m}  \times \mathbb{Z}_{n}$ by $f( k )=( k\text{ mod }m, k\text{ mod }n )$
> 2. This is an isomorphism of rings:
> 	1. $f$ is a bijection under both operations
> 	2. $f$ satisfies the homomorphic property for both operations:
> 		1. $f( j+k )=f( j )+f( k )$
> 		2. $f( jk )=f( j )f( k )$
