> [!definition]
> If a [[Rings|ring]] has an [[Identity Element|identity element]] for $\cdot$, it is called $1$, or **unity**.
> A ring with such an element is a *ring with unity*.

> [!remark]
> All of the examples of rings we wrote above *are* rings with unity.

> [!example]
> ##### Examples *with* unity
> - $M_{n}( \mathbb{R} )$, the unity is $1=I$
> - $\mathcal{P}( U)$, the unity is $1=U$
> 	- $X\cap U=X$
> 	- $U \cap X=X$
> ##### Example *without* unity
> - $E$ with $+$ and $\cdot$ where $E=\left\{ ...,-4,-2,0,2,4,... \right\}$
