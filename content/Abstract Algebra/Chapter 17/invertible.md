> [!definition]
> A [[Rings|ring]] element $a$ is **invertible** if it has a multiplicative inverse, i.e. some element $b$ such that $ab=1$ and $ba=1$.

> [!remark]
> An invertible element *cannot* be a [[zero divisors|zero divisor]].

> [!proof]
> 1. Suppose toward a contradiction $a$ is invertible and $a$ is a zero divisor
> 2. Then $aa^{-1}=1$ and $a^{-1}a=1$ while $ab=0$ for some $b\ne 0$
> 	1. then $a^{-1}ab=a^{-1}\cdot0$
> 	2. so $b=0$, *a contradiction.*
> 3. So $a\ne 0$ and $a$ is a zero divisor
> - Note, the converse of the corollary is also true.
