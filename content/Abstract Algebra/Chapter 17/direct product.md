> [!definition]
> If $A$ and $B$ are [[rings]], then their **direct product** is: $$A \times B=\left\{ ( x,y )\ |\ x\in A\text{ and }y\in  B \right\}$$... where $( x,y )+( z,t )=( x+z ),( y+t )$
> ... and $( x,y )( z,t )=( xz,yt )$

- The direct product is also a ring.
- The (nontrivial) direct product can *never* be an integral domain or a field.

> [!remark]
> The [[direct product]] of [[Fields|fields]] is *not* a field. Moreover, the direct product of integral domains is *not* an integral domain.
