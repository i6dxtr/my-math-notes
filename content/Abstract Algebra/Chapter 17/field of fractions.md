> [!remark]
> Let $B$ be a commutative ring with [[unity]]. If $B$ is a [[subring]] of some [[Fields|field]], then $B$ is an [[integral domain]]. 
> Alternatively, every field is an integral domain and every subring with unity of an integral domain is an integral domain.
>
> Conversely, if $B$ is an integral domain, *then* it is a subring of some field, called its **field of fractions**.
>
> This fact generalizes this prototypical example:

> [!corollary] Fact.
> $\mathbb{Z}$ is an integral domain, and $\mathbb{Z}$ is a subring of the field $\mathbb{Q}$.
