> [!theorem] Theorem. (B)
> Let $a,b\in G$ where $G$ is a [[Groups|group]]. Then $(ab)^{-1}=b^{-1}a^{-1}$

> [!proof] Proof. (B)
> WTS: $ab$ and $b^{-1}a^{-1}$ are inverses of each other 
> $$
> > \begin{align}
> abb^{-1}a^{-1}&=aea^{-1}=aa^{-1}&=e. \\
> b^{-1}a^{-1}ab&=b^{-1}eb=b^{-1}b&=e.
> \end{align}
>
> $$
