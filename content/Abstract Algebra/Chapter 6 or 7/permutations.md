- [[Functions]] may map from a set to itself
	- $f:A\rightarrow A$
- two such functions can be equal
	- $f(x)=g(x)$  $\forall x\in G\longrightarrow f=g$
- a **permutation** of a set $A$ is a *[[bijective]] function* $f:A\rightarrow A$
- permutations can be written as representations of [[bijective|one-to-one correspondence]] relations
	 ![[Pasted image 20240925140213.png]]
	- where $\left\{ 3,2,1,5,4 \right\}$ is a permutation of $\left\{ 1,2,3,4,5 \right\}$
- in other words, any permutation on a set $A$ can be expressed as a one-to-one correspondence from $\mathbb{Z}^{+}$

> [!remark]
> The [[composition|composite]] of any two permutations of $A$ is a permutation of $A$. Therefore, the operation $\circ$ of composition *is an operation on the set of all the permutations of $A$.*

- note that $\circ$ is an associative operation

- the inverse of any permutation of $A$ is a permutation of $A$
