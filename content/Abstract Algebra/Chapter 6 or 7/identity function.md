> [!definition]
> For any set $A$, the **identity function** on $A$, symbolized with $\epsilon_{A}$ or simply $\epsilon$, is the [[function]] $x\rightarrow x$ which carries every element of $A$ to itself. In other words, $$\epsilon(x)=x\text{ for every element }x\in  A$$ 

- since $\epsilon$ is a [[permutations|permutation]] of $A$ and $f$ is any other permutation of $A$, we say:

> [!remark]
> $$
> > \begin{align}
> f\circ \epsilon=f &&\text{and}&&\epsilon\circ f=f
> \end{align}
>
> $$
