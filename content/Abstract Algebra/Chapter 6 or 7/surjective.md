> [!definition]
> A function $f\ :\ A\rightarrow B$ is called **surjective** if each element of $B$ is the image of at least one element of $A$.
> ![[Pasted image 20240924142406.png]]

- e.g. $B$ is in the range of $f$.

- the opposite phenomena is [[injective|injectivity]]
- if $f$ is both surjective and injective, it is [[bijective]]
