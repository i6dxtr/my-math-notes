> [!definition]
> A [[Functions|function]] $f : A\rightarrow B$ if each element of $B$ is the image of no more than one element of $A$.
> $$x_{1}, x_{2}\longrightarrow y=f(x_{1})=f(x_{2})$$
> ![[Pasted image 20240924142228.png]]

> [!remark]
> A function $f : A\rightarrow B$ is injective *if and only if*:
> $$f(x_{1})=f(x_{2})\longrightarrow x_{1}=x_{2}$$

- aka, *one-to-one*
- the opposite phenomena is [[surjective|surjectivity]]
- if $f$ is both surjective and injective, it is [[bijective]].
