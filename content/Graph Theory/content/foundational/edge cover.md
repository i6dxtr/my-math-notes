> ### Definition — Edge cover
> Let $G$ be a graph and let $L\subseteq E(G)$. We say that $L$ is an **edge cover** of $G$ if every vertex of $G$ is incident with at least one edge in $L$. Let $\beta'(G)$ denote the size of a minimum edge cover of $G$. If $G$ has an isolated vertex then, by convention, set $\beta'(G)=\infty$.
