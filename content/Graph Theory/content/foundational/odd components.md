> [!definition]
> ### Definition 3.3.1 — Odd components and Tutte's condition
> Let $H$ be a graph. Denote by $o(H)$ the number of odd components of $H$ (components with an odd number of vertices).
> 
> We say that a graph $G$ satisfies Tutte's condition if
> $$
> o\bigl(G-S\bigr)\le \lvert S\rvert \qquad\text{for every } S\subseteq V(G).
> $$
> In words: for every vertex subset $S$, the number of odd components of the subgraph obtained by deleting $S$ is at most the size of $S$.

### Relations
- [[matching]] — Tutte's condition is a necessary and (together with Tutte's theorem) sufficient condition for the existence of perfect matchings; see matching definitions and notation.
- [[theorem - Tutte's theorem]] — Tutte's theorem uses this definition as its main hypothesis/characterization.
