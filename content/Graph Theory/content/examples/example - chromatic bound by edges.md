> [!example]
> ### Chromatic bound by edges — $\chi(G)<1+\sqrt{2m}$
> Let $G$ be a graph with $m$ edges. Then
> $$\chi(G)<1+\sqrt{2m}.$$ 
> [!proof]
> Let $k:=\chi(G)$. By Turán-type counting (or a simple double-counting/inequality argument), the number of edges satisfies
> $$m\ge\binom{k}{2}=\frac{k(k-1)}{2}>\frac{(k-1)^2}{2}.$$ 
> Solving for $k$ gives
> $$k-1<\sqrt{2m}\quad\Rightarrow\quad k<1+\sqrt{2m},$$
> as required.
