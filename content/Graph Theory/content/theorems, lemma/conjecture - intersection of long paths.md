> [!theorem]
> ### Conjecture — Intersection of Three Longest Paths
> For every graph $G$, if $P$, $Q$, and $R$ are paths of maximum length (i.e., longest) in $G$, then $V(P)\cap V(Q)\cap V(R)\neq\varnothing$.
> [!remark]
> - For two longest paths, intersection is guaranteed in any connected graph; see [[exercise - 7 maximal vs maximum paths]].
> - The statement above strengthens to three longest paths and is formulated here as a conjecture consistent with the lecture notes.
