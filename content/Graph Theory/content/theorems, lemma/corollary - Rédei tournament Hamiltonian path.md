> [!corollary]
> ### Rédei (1934) — Every tournament has a Hamiltonian path
> Every tournament has a Hamiltonian path.

> [!proof]
> A tournament on $n$ vertices is an orientation of the complete graph $K_n$. By Gallai–Roy–Vitaver (Theorem), for some orientation $D$ of $K_n$ we have
> $$\\chi(K_n)\le l(D)+1.$$ Since $\\chi(K_n)=n$, this implies $l(D)\ge n-1$, so $D$ contains a directed path of length $n-1$, i.e. a Hamiltonian path in the tournament.
