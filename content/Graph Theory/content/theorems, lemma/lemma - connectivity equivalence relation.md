> [!corollary]
> ### Lemma 0.1.
> Given a graph $G,$ let $\sim$ be a relation on $V( G )$ where $u\sim v$ *if and only if* there is a $u,v-$path in $G.$ We have that $\sim$ is an equivalence relation and the equivalence classes are the components of $G.$
### Importance & Implications
This lemma provides a rigorous, algebraic foundation for the concept of [[connectedness|connected components]]. It formally justifies the idea that a graph is partitioned into its components based on which vertices are reachable from each other.
