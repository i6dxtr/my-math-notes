### Contrast for images
- $A$ is a matrix
- $M$ = average over all entries of $A$
	- $0 \leq S \leq 1$: decrease contrast
	- $1 \leq S$: increase contrast
- Make a new image:
$$A_{new}=sA+(1-s)m$$
- *Claim*: $s >1$ "increases contrast"
	- Draw $f(x)=sx+(1-s)m$
	-          $f(M)=sm+(1-s)m=m$
	- slope of $f$ is $s$
	- $sa+(1-s)m >a$ when $a<M$
	- transformation function vals higher after point M, lower before
- If $s<1$
	- transformation function vals lower after point M, higher before

## Section 1.1, 1.2
- Solving systems of linear equations
	1. $3x_{1}-7x_{2}=8$
	 $5x_{1}+x_{2}=1$
	2. Rewriting in vector form:
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
3x_{1}-7x_{2} \\
5x_{1}+x_{2}
\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}
8 \\
1
\end{pmatrix}
$$
	3. Rewrite as matrix multiplication:
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & -7 \\
5 & 1
\end{pmatrix}\cdot\begin{pmatrix}
x_{1} \\
x_{2}
\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}
8 \\
1
\end{pmatrix}
$$
	4. Fill the "augmented matrix" (too much junk)
$$
\left(\begin{array}{cc|c}
 3 & -7 & 8 \\
5 & 1 & 1 
\end{array}\right)
$$
	5. Solve resulting matrix (starting with above using row operations).
$$
\left(\begin{array}{cc|c}
 3 & -7 & 8 \\
5 & 1 & 1 
\end{array}\right)\longrightarrow ^{R_{3}\rightarrow R_{2}, \frac{5}{3}R_{1}} 
\left(\begin{array}{cc|c}
 3 & -7 & 8 \\
0 & \frac{38}{3} & -\frac{37}{3} 
\end{array}\right)
$$
$$
\begin{align}
3x_{1}-7x_{2}&= 8 \\
\frac{38}{3}x_{2}=-\frac{37}{3} \\
x_{2}&-=\frac{37}{38} \\
x_{1}&=\frac{8+7\left( -\frac{37}{38} \right)}{3}
\end{align}
$$

> [!remark]
> **3 row operations**:
> - Swap any two rows
> - Scalar multiplication by a non-zero scalar
> - Add a scalar multiple of one row to another
