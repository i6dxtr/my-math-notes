> [!definition]
> Suppose the [[Vector Space|vector space]] $V$ has a [[basis]] $\mathbf{v}_{1}, ..., \mathbf{v}_{n}$ for some $n\in N$. Then every other basis of $V$ has the same number, $n$, of elements in it. This number is called the **dimension** of $V$, and written $\text{dim}(V)=n$.

> [!corollary]
> Suppose $\mathbf{v}_{1}, ..., \mathbf{v}_{n}$ span a vector space $V$. Then every set of $k>n$ elements $\mathbf{w}_{1}, ..., \mathbf{w}_{k}$ is linearly dependent.

- so no matrix with less rows than columns can have [[linear independence]]
