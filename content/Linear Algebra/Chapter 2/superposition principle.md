> [!theorem] Theorem: Superposition Principle
> If $\mathbf{z}_{1}, ..., \mathbf{z}_{k}$ are individual solutions to the same homogeneous linear system $A\mathbf{z}=\mathbf{0}$, then so is every linear combination $c_{1}\mathbf{z}_{1}+...+c_{k}\mathbf{z}_{k}$.

- note, set of inhomogeneous solutions $A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}\ :\ \mathbf{b}\neq 0$ is *not* a subspace
- arises from fact that $A\mathbf{z}=\mathbf{0}=A\mathbf{w}$ where $\mathbf{z}, \mathbf{w}\in \text{ker}\ {A}$
	- thus is dependent on the [[kernel space]]
