### Interior Point
> [!definition]
> Let $( X, d )$ be a [[Metric (space)|metric space]] and $E$ is a subset of $X$. A point $e\in E$ is called an **interior point** of $E$ if *there exists* $\varepsilon>0$ such that $$N_{\varepsilon}(e)\subset E$$... holds.
