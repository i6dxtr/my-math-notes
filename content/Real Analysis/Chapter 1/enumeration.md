> [!definition]
> If $A$ is [[cardinality|countable]], then there is a [[function]] $f:\mathbb{N}\rightarrow A$ that is both *one-to-one* and *onto*: $$A=\left\{ f( n ):n\in  \mathbb{N} \right\}=\left\{ x_{n}, n\in  \mathbb{N} \right\}$$... such $\left\{ x_{n}, n\in \mathbb{N} \right\}$ is called an **enumeration** of $A$.
