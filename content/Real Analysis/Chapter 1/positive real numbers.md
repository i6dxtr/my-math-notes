### Positive Real Numbers
> [!definition]
> The real numbers contain a [[sets|subset]] $\mathbb{P}$ known as the **positive real numbers.**

> [!corollary]
> ### Postulates of the positive reals
> 1. If $a,b\in \mathbb{P}$ then $a+b$ and $a+b\in \mathbb{P}$
> 2. If $a\in \mathbb{R}$ then *one and only one* of the following hold:
> 	1. $a\in \mathbb{P}$
> 	2. $-a\in \mathbb{P}$
> 	3. $a=0$.

> [!remark]
> For the rationals $\mathbb{Q}$, the set of **positive rational numbers** is given by $\mathbb{P}\cap \mathbb{Q}=\left\{ \frac{p}{q}: p,q\in \mathbb{Z},q\neq 0, pq\in \mathbb{N} \right\}$
- 1 and 2 are called **order properties**.
	- Any field $\mathbb{F}$ satisfying the order properties is called an **ordered field**.
- For $a,b\in \mathbb{R}$, we write $a>b$ (or $b<a$) if $a-b\in \mathbb{P}$
> [!corollary]
> ### More properties of positive real numbers
> 1. If $a>b$ then $a+c>b+c$
> 2. If $a>b$ and $c\in \mathbb{P}$, then $ac>bc$
> 3. If $a>b$ and $c<0$, then $ac<bc$
> 4. If $a\neq 0$, then $a^{2}>0$
> 5. Finally, if $a>0$, then $\frac{1}{a}>0$.
