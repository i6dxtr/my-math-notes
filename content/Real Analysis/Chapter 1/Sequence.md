### Sequence
> [!definition]
> Let $A$ be a [[set]]. A **sequence** is given as: $$f:\mathbb{N}\longrightarrow A$$... where $f( n )=x_{n}\in A$.
> We can write this as $\left\{ x_{n} \right\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ *or* $\left\{ x_{n} \right\}_{n\ge 1}$ *or* $\left\{ x_{n} \right\}$
