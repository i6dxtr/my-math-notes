#### 3.6 q12
*Given:*
- $a_{1}\in ( 0,1 )$
- $a_{n+1}=\frac{1}{5}( 1-a_{n}^{3} )$
*Is the sequence contractive?*
- $a_{n+1}-a_{n}=\frac{1}{5}( 1-a_{n}^{3} )-\frac{1}{5}( 1-a_{n-1}^{3} )=\frac{1}{5}( a_{n-1}^{3}-a_{n}^{3} )$....
- $\lvert a_{n+1}-a_{n} \rvert=\frac{1}{5}\lvert a_{n}-a_{n-1} \rvert\left[ a_{n}^{2}+a_{n}a_{n-1}+a_{n-1}^{2} \right]$
- We know $a_{1}\in ( 0,1 )$
- We'll show $0\le a_{n}\le 1$
- Assume that $0\le a_{k}\le 1$, which is true iff $0\le a_{k}^{3}\le 1$
- So $0\le 1-a_{k}^{3}\le 1$
- So $a_{k+1}\le \frac{1}{5}( 1-a_{k}^{3} )\le \frac{1}{5}\le 1.$
- Implying $\lvert a_{n+1}-a_{n} \rvert\le \frac{1}{5}( 1^{2}+1^{2}+1^{2} )\lvert a_{n}-a_{n-1} \rvert$
- therefore $$\lvert a_{n+1}-a_{n} \rvert \le \frac{3}{5}\lvert a_{n}-a_{n-1} \rvert .$$

*Prove that $f( x )=\sqrt{x}$ is continuous on $(0,1].$*
- Fix $p\in (0,1]$
- We show $\forall\varepsilon>0 \exists \delta>0$ such that if $\forall x\in (0,1]$ with $\lvert x-p \rvert<\delta$ then $\left\lvert  \frac{1}{\sqrt{x}}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{p}}  \right\rvert<\varepsilon$
- Rewrite in terms of $x-p:$
	$$
	\begin{align} \left\lvert  \frac{1}{\sqrt{x}}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{p}}  \right\rvert &=\frac{\lvert \sqrt{x}- \sqrt{p} \rvert }{\sqrt{x}\sqrt{p}} \\ &=\frac{\lvert x-p \rvert }{\sqrt{x}\sqrt{p}( \sqrt{x}+\sqrt{p} )} \end{align}
	$$
- Estimate denominator: Choose $\frac{\sqrt{p}}{2}$ such that $\frac{\sqrt{p}}{2}<\sqrt{x}$
	$$
	\begin{align} \left\lvert  \frac{1}{\sqrt{x}}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{p}}  \right\rvert &<\frac{\lvert x-p \rvert }{\sqrt{\frac{p}{2}}\sqrt{p}\left(  \sqrt{\frac{p}{2}}+\sqrt{p}  \right)}\\&<\varepsilon \end{align}
	$$
- Take $\delta=\text{min}\left\{ \frac{p}{2}, \varepsilon\left(  \sqrt{\frac{p}{2}}\sqrt{p}\left(  \sqrt{\frac{p}{2}}+\sqrt{p}  \right)  \right) \right\}$
- If $\lvert x-p \rvert<\delta$ then $\left\lvert  \frac{1}{\sqrt{p}}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{p}}  \right\rvert<\varepsilon$
